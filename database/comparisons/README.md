# MathBridge comparison notes

These files contain the short teacher-facing comparison layer.

Each record answers three questions:

1. **What is different?** — curriculum timing, emphasis or documented representation/guidance difference.
2. **Why might the lesson look different?** — a narrow pedagogical explanation supported by curriculum/guidance/research. Do not infer national teaching culture.
3. **What can a Chinese teacher use?** — one practical option that complements the Chinese curriculum.

Rules:

- China uses the 2022 national standard at learning-stage level unless current PEP grade/semester evidence is verified.
- England means the National Curriculum in England plus clearly labelled DfE/NCETM guidance.
- US means Common Core baseline unless a state framework is explicitly selected.
- Earlier/later curriculum timing is descriptive, not a quality judgement.
- A teaching representation is not described as a national norm unless the evidence supports that claim.
- Copy is direct and educational. No marketing language.
