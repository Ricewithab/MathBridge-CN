# MathBridge Copy Style

Use plain educational language.

## Write like this

- State the mathematical idea directly.
- Explain what the teacher should do.
- Explain what the pupil should notice or understand.
- Use short headings.
- Use short examples where they help.
- Label evidence clearly: `Verified`, `Partially verified`, or `Research required`.
- Keep Chinese translations natural and professional.

## Avoid

- marketing language
- product claims
- slogans
- hype
- exaggerated benefits
- vague phrases such as “transform learning” or “unlock potential”
- unnecessary adjectives
- AI-style filler
- rhetorical introductions when a direct explanation will do

## Example

Avoid:

> This powerful visual strategy helps unlock a deeper understanding of multiplication.

Use:

> Use an array to show equal groups and the relationship between rows, columns and the product.

The purpose of MathBridge copy is to explain curriculum, mathematics and teaching decisions clearly.
